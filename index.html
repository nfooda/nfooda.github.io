About

I'm a fourth year Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, advised by Prof. Dave Levin. My research is in web privacy and network security, with a focus on the Web PKI: I measure how quickly certificate revocations reach browsers, evaluate how Firefox checks revocation at scale with CRLite, and study how privacy technologies such as DNS over HTTPS and TLS are adopted in real-world browsers.

Research

Certificate Revocation Latency

Measuring how long it takes for a revoked certificate to stop being trusted. Issuing and immediately revoking certificates, then tracking when the revocation appears in each CA's OCSP responses, CRLs, and CRLite, to quantify the window of vulnerability across major CAs. Now extending the study to the long tail of CAs.

PKIOCSP / CRLMeasurementPython
Evaluating Modern Certificate Revocation (CRLite)

Measuring how PKI changes since 2017 (shorter certificate lifetimes, the rise of Let's Encrypt) affect large-scale revocation. Evaluating CRLite filter generation and partitioning strategies under normal and mass-revocation events, and quantifying the window of vulnerability and bandwidth overhead.

PKITLSBrowser securityMeasurement
Privacy-Technology Adoption in Firefox

Designing and building a Firefox extension that logs users' privacy settings and browser behavior to study real-world adoption of DNS over HTTPS, TLS, and revocation checking.
