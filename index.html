About

I'm a Ph.D. student at the University of Maryland, advised by Prof. Dave Levin. My research is in web privacy and network security: I measure how privacy-preserving technologies such as DNS over HTTPS, TLS, and certificate revocation actually perform and get adopted in real-world browsers.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a software engineer intern at Siemens, building C/C++ tooling and internal dashboards, and I have hands-on experience in full-stack development, hardware security, and applied ML/NLP.

Research

Evaluating Modern Certificate Revocation (CRLite)

Measuring how PKI changes since 2017 (shorter certificate lifetimes, the rise of Let's Encrypt) affect large-scale revocation. Evaluating CRLite filter generation and partitioning strategies under normal and mass-revocation events, and quantifying the window of vulnerability and bandwidth overhead.
